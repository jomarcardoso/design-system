Water.css adapter

Classless, and the smallest surface here: twenty-one variables. Water has no notion of a primary action — its buttons are grey chrome and the accent lives only in links — so this adapter changes that on purpose.

Buttons

Water sets only background-color on buttons and lets the text inherit --text-main. That is fine on the grey it ships with and dark-on-dark on a real brand colour, so the adapter binds the foreground on the property.

Every button Water documents

Water has no variant classes at all — it styles four selectors and stops. That is the whole button surface, and it is why the adapter binds the foreground on the property: there is no --button-text to point at.

A link, which Water styles as text rather than a button
What this page is testing:
  • A library with no brand fill. --button-base is a grey. Water models buttons as neutral chrome, so pointing it at bg-action is a real change to how Water looks — and the right one, because a <button> is the canonical action and a design system whose brand never reaches it is not doing its job.
  • A third library needing the pair exported. After Bootstrap and Flowbite: Water has no --button-text, so the foreground half is bound on the property. Every time a library omits it, the same failure appears — a fill the theme controls and text it does not.
  • The floor holds. Twenty-one variables, no variant classes, no namespace. If the four layers can drive this cleanly they can drive almost anything — which is the point of testing against the smallest surface as well as the largest.
